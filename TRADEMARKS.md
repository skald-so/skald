# Trademarks

"Skald" and the Skald logo are trademarks of the Skald maintainer. The code licenses do not grant rights to them; this policy does.

## Allowed without permission
- Refer to Skald truthfully: "works with Skald", "a plugin for Skald", "based on Skald".
- Use the name in articles, talks, tutorials, reviews and documentation.
- Redistribute unmodified official builds under the Skald name, including in package managers with patches needed only to build or install.
- Run Skald, modified or not, for yourself or your organization.

## Needs written permission
- Distributing a modified version, a fork or a hosted service under the Skald name or logo. Rename it and drop the logo; "based on Skald" is fine.
- Using "Skald" or a confusingly similar name in a product, company, domain or package name, e.g. `skald-*` on crates.io, npm or PyPI.
- Implying that Skald endorses or is affiliated with you.
- Changing the logo or combining it with other marks.

## Asking for permission
Open an issue in [skald-so/skald](https://github.com/skald-so/skald/issues).
